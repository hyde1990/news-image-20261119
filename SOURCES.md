## Lead — Diplomacy, Shenzhen

Donald Trump, Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin hold the first-ever three-way
summit in Shenzhen, on the sidelines of APEC. About 90 minutes, no
immediate breakthrough. Joint pledge: talk further on nuclear risks,
reform the UN, boost oil production. Ukraine was notably not the main
topic in their joint declaration, per the Kremlin spokesman. Trump also
held separate bilaterals with Putin (about 40 minutes) and Xi (about an
hour). Zelensky warned it could be a "new Yalta".
Sources: Reuters, AP, AFP, BBC, The Times.

## Card 1 — Justice, The Hague

The International Court of Justice rules Myanmar committed genocide
against the Rohingya in its 2017 military operations, finding genocidal
intent to destroy a substantial part of the minority. Evidence showed
about 10,000 Rohingya killed; over 730,000 fled to Bangladesh. Case
brought by The Gambia in 2019 under the 1948 Genocide Convention. Final,
binding judgment, read by court president Yuji Iwasawa.
Sources: United Nations / International Court of Justice, Reuters, AP,
BBC, Al Jazeera.

## Card 2 — War in Ukraine

Ukraine strikes the Kerch Strait Bridge for a third time in a week; road
and rail traffic suspended after the road span collapsed earlier in the
week.
Sources: Reuters, AP, AFP.

## Card 3 — Conflict, The Gulf

Iran attacks Gulf states after US strikes on Qeshm Island; Kuwait repels
its first Iranian attack in two months. A Houthi rocket damages the PIF
Tower in Riyadh's financial district (debris after interception).
Sources: Reuters, AP, AFP, Al Jazeera. The IEA angle (Hormuz) is in the
ticker; source: IEA.

## Card 4 — Bangladesh

Parts of Bangladesh shut down in a nationwide lockdown called by the
banned Awami League ahead of former PM Sheikh Hasina's planned return in
December. The ancestral home of former interim leader Muhammad Yunus in
Chattogram is set ablaze late on Wednesday; his family upstairs are safe.
Police say two ruling party members were killed; at least 37 Grameen Bank
branches targeted in arson since 10 November.
Sources: Reuters, AP, AFP, BBC.

## Card 5 — Tech, Markets

Sources: Reuters, AP, USA Today.

## Card 6 — Culture, Games

Grand Theft Auto VI launches on Thursday 19 November, after a 13-year

characters: Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos.
Sources: SensorTower, Reuters, AP, BBC.
